faro.raw

faro is a traditional artist working in graphite and shadow.

Her work lives in the space between horror and tenderness — where the body becomes a language, and every wound a word. She draws what cannot be said: limerence, body horror, dark love, the medieval weight of grief, the quiet violence of feeling too much.

Black and white, mostly. Occasionally, red.

She is not interested in decoration. She is interested in what happens when something breaks open.
